lxrogers / jewelryproto

Jewelry Proto Bench

Twelve browser prototypes for designing rings and coins in 3D. Each one renders gold under the same softbox HDRI, with sliders for the shape. The B-rep demos load a 10 MB OpenCascade kernel, so give them a few seconds on first open.
